The person behind the paintbrush

Meet Tanvina

Founder, lead instructor, and the artist behind every original piece on the studio walls.

Artist & instructor

Painting has always been the way I slow down.

Tanvina has spent years developing a detailed, patient watercolor practice — the same florals, still-life studies, and architectural pieces you see throughout the studio and gallery are her own original work.

She founded Maple Art Gallery to share that same sense of focus and quiet confidence with students of every age, believing that anyone — a five-year-old holding a brush for the first time, or an adult picking one up again after years away — can learn to really see, and really paint.

Her teaching style is hands-on and encouraging: less about getting it "right," and more about building the skills and patience to keep going.

— Tanvina

🎓 Formally trained in watercolor & fine art 🖼️ Exhibited original works 👩‍🏫 7+ years teaching kids & adults

Our philosophy

What we believe about learning art

🌱

Everyone starts somewhere

There's no "art person" and "non-art person" — just people who've had guidance, and people who haven't yet.

🔍

Technique builds confidence

Real skills — color mixing, composition, brush control — make creativity feel possible, not intimidating.

🤍

Process over perfection

A finished piece matters less than the patience, focus, and care that went into making it.
