Meet your instructor

Taught by an artist who still paints every day

Tanvina founded Maple Art Gallery to share the quiet, focused joy of watercolor with students of every age. Her own botanical and still-life work — much of it on display around the studio — shapes the way she teaches: patiently, hands-on, and always encouraging.

Every class blends real technique with room to make it your own.
